## 短介绍

**中文**
一款 3D 推线小游戏。炮里打出来的是圆滚滚的波波，穿过「+N」「×N」的门就变多，踩到减号门就变少。一路挤过机关，撞开对面的大门。七个甜点种族，打法都不一样。打开网页就能玩，不用登录。

**English**
A 3D lane pusher. Your cannon fires round little jellies. "+N" and "×N" gates grow the crowd, minus gates shrink it. Squeeze past the machines and knock down the far gate. Seven dessert tribes, seven play styles. Runs in the browser, no account. Interface in Simplified Chinese only.

## 长介绍

**中文**
左右拖动，对准门，开炮。

有的门会移动。有加的、乘的、减的、除的，还有变大门和冰盾门。

每五关一个小首领，每十关一个大首领。有护盾副塔的，先拆副塔。

七个种族各有一棵技能树：波波族会分裂，布丁族会合体，兔耳族让门更划算，喵喵族放电，云朵族放礼花，曲奇族守得住，招财族多拿赏金。五种炮可以换着用。

开打前编一组三张战术卡，到点自己触发。

累了就去无尽工坊保卫战守几波，或者玩十几个花样关：雪橇、潜艇、列车、金字塔、藤蔓秋千。

每周一张榜。真人排前面，机器人标「陪练」，不占名次。

皮肤扭蛋只用游戏里打来的金币和晶核，几率和保底写在机器上。目前免费，没有充值，没有广告。

**English**
Drag left and right, line up the gate, fire.

Some gates move. There are add, multiply, subtract and divide gates, a giant gate and an ice shield gate.

A small boss every five stages, a big one every ten. Where there are shield towers, take those out first.

Seven tribes with their own skill trees: splitters, mergers, gate boosters, electric cats, firework clouds, sturdy cookies, and bounty hunters. Five cannons to swap between.

Pick three tactic cards before a fight. They trigger by themselves.

For a break, defend the workshop wave by wave, or try a dozen side stages with sleds, submarines, trains, pyramids and vines.

There is a weekly board. Real players come first, bots are marked as practice partners and take no rank.

The skin capsule machine only takes coins and cores earned in play. Odds and pity are printed on it. Free, no purchases, no ads. Interface in Simplified Chinese only.
