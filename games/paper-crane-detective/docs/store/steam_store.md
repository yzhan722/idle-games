## 短简介（中文，商店首屏）

她负责看见真相，你负责说出真相。转学第一天就蒙冤的你，遇见了一位从不开口的侦探少女。五种结局、约 15 万字的校园推理视觉小说。

## 长简介（中文）

转学第一天，你在图书馆被当成了嫌疑人。

替你洗清冤屈的，是坐在窗边、一个字也不说的女孩墨墨。她看得见所有人漏掉的细节，却只肯写在笔记本上。于是你成了她的「嘴」——在一间叫「万事屋」的活动室里，和精打细算的社长艾丽丝一起，接下校园里一桩又一桩委托。

四年前，她为什么停止说话？答案就藏在你们破的每一个案子里。

**调查与推理**
- 问询、勘查、收集证物，证物可以翻到背面看批注
- 从几条说法里找出真正的矛盾，或把两条线索拼成一个推论
- 猜错会消耗「直觉」，失误太多会走向坏结局

**五种结局**
- 序章、五章、终章与两段日常间章，约 15 万字
- 选择会影响羁绊与线索；五种结局（含一段隐藏后日谈）都可以在鉴赏页重温

**案卷：两篇番外与八桩可重玩短案**
- 八桩短案难度相同：6 条线索、4 步推理、每步 3 选 1
- 同一套评级：零失误金牌，失误 1～2 次银牌，3 次及以上铜牌；失误不影响主线，可以反复挑战

**鉴赏**
- 42 张 CG、13 首配乐、40 项成就
- 委托信箱、午后茶话会、古籍修复台、四季信笺匣、旧广播电台、钟楼星象馆等小玩法

## 短简介（English）

She sees the truth; you say it out loud. Framed on your first day at a new school, you meet a girl detective who never speaks. A campus-mystery visual novel with five endings. (Simplified Chinese text only.)

## 长简介（English）

On your first day at a new school you become the prime suspect in the library.

The girl who clears your name is Momo, who sits by the window and does not say a word. She notices what everyone else misses, and writes it down in a notebook. You become her voice. Together with Alice, the club president who keeps a ledger of every favour, you take on requests from around the campus.

Why did she stop speaking four years ago? The answer is hidden in the cases you solve.

- **Investigate and deduce** – question people, search scenes, flip evidence over to read the notes on the back, pick out the contradiction, or combine two clues into a conclusion.
- **Five endings** – a prologue, five chapters, a finale and two interludes; about 150,000 Chinese characters; every ending can be replayed from the gallery.
- **Case files** – two bonus stories and eight replayable short cases, all at the same difficulty (6 clues, 4 deduction steps, 3 choices each) and rated the same way (gold: no mistakes, silver: 1–2, bronze: 3 or more).
- **Gallery** – 42 CGs, 13 music tracks, 40 achievements, and several small activities.

The game is in Simplified Chinese. 42 lines are voiced in Chinese. There is no English translation.
