## 长介绍

**中文**
你在异世界醒来，手里只有一把斧头。先砍树，再做木料，再换一把好点的斧头。

六种手艺各有采集和制作两条线。每个动作写明要多久、给多少经验、出什么、几成机会出。等级不够的也看得见，知道下一级能买到什么。

打架是自动的。技能什么时候放、血掉到多少吃东西，你来定。十一片战区，每片有普通、困难、噩梦三档。

装备能强化。成功率写在按钮旁边，失败会怎样也先告诉你。

市场是玩家之间的。系统给一个估价和可挂的区间，不会一不小心把东西贱卖。

想结伴就组队。队伍六个人，人不够可以找电脑队友，电脑队友会标出来。

说清楚三件事：要注册账号，要联网；关掉页面后进度会停住，不算离线收益；目前免费，没有付费内容。

**English**
You wake up in another world with an axe. Chop wood, plane it, buy a better axe.

Each of the six trades has a gathering side and a crafting side. Every action shows its time, XP, output and odds. Locked actions stay visible, so you always know what the next level brings.

Combat runs by itself. You decide when skills fire and when food is eaten. There are eleven zones with three difficulty tiers each.

Gear can be enhanced. The success rate is printed next to the button, and so is what happens when it fails.

The market is between players. The game suggests a price and a listing range.

Parties hold up to six. If you are short of people you can add computer teammates, and they are labelled as such.

Three things to know: you need an account and a connection; progress stops when you close the page, so there is no offline income; the game is free and sells nothing for now. Interface in Simplified Chinese only.
